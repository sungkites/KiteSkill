# Kite Writing / 论文写作与润色

Read [the writing modes](references/writing.md) for substantial restructuring,
translation or journal adaptation. Use journal instructions and a user-supplied
reference article as style/organization evidence, not as content to imitate.

## Establish the evidence boundary

Identify the requested section, edit scope, audience/venue and output language.
Read the supplied results, figures and source material. Build a small claim–evidence
map when drafting a full section or paper; for a sentence edit, avoid extra process.
Do not invent results, effect sizes, citations, institutional approvals or completed
experiments. If evidence is missing, narrow the prose or mark the missing dependency
outside the manuscript; do not silently turn a proposed study into completed work.

## Choose the actual task

- **Draft**: organize the scientific question, mechanism/method, decisive evidence,
  comparison and interpretation. Use the venue's section conventions.
- **Restructure**: repair the argument and claim order while retaining valid facts,
  citations and uncertainty. Explain material claim changes to the author.
- **Polish**: improve precision, grammar, flow and terminology within the selected
  scope. Preserve numbers, units, negation, causality, certainty and source support.
- **Translate**: render meaning naturally in the requested language; resolve ambiguous
  terms from context and keep unresolved scientific ambiguities visible to the author.

## Write a research paper

Use concise, natural scholarly prose. Foreground the question, method and evidence.
Introduce why a method component is needed and what mechanism it tests. Relate
experiments to claims and explain the supported interpretation and scope.
Avoid generic AI openings, inflated novelty claims, repetitive defensive language
and unexplained module lists. Keep setup/troubleshooting/command logs in separate
reproduction files. Use lists or structured abstracts where the venue calls for them.

For strong-journal aspirations, assess the actual contribution and evidence; do not
claim acceptance or Q1 readiness from wording or formatting. Match conference,
biomedical and journal conventions to the actual venue rather than imposing one
universal paper template. Nature-style writing does not imply Nature affiliation.

## Verify and deliver

Check numerical/terminological consistency across text, tables, figures and captions.
Check citations against available records and passages; use `kite-literature` if
available, otherwise perform focused checks and label unresolved ones. For Word or
LaTeX deliverables, use available document/compilation tools and inspect rendering;
if only text is requested, do not generate extra document formats.

Return the requested prose first. Add a short change/evidence note only if useful.
If a whole manuscript needs revision, preserve untouched sections and current author
changes. Do not replace an entire document because a selected paragraph was supplied.
