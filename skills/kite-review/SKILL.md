# Kite Review / 投稿审查与修回

Select review, targeted audit or response mode. Read [the review protocol](references/review.md)
for full manuscripts, statistical concerns or point-by-point response packages.
Use only supplied/authorized manuscripts and sources.

## Review mode

Identify venue and article type if available. Inspect claims, figures, methods,
results and supplementary material. Prioritize scientific validity and contribution
before prose. For each material concern, give the exact location, supporting
evidence, consequence and smallest useful remedy. Distinguish a confirmed defect
from missing information, a tentative concern and personal presentation preference.

Assess closest-work novelty, baseline fairness, leakage, replication, uncertainty,
causal language, mechanism evidence and source support. Check
what the supplied artifacts actually show. Do not invent a methodological failure
from absent context; request or identify the missing artifact specifically.

An editorial/technical/statistical/skeptical perspective can help organize a review,
but is not independent evidence or a guaranteed acceptance prediction. Do not spawn
multiple agents unless authorized by the user or applicable instructions.

## Response and revision mode

Preserve every editor/reviewer point and create a concern → action → evidence →
manuscript location map. Draft a respectful direct answer. State what changed and
where, or give a source-grounded reason for disagreement. Separate proposed actions
from completed work; never claim an experiment, analysis or edit was performed
until its artifact is verified. Do not invent line numbers or result improvements.

Implement authorized revisions and check consistency between response letter,
manuscript, figures, references and supplements. If a concern requires unavailable
data or substantial new compute, deliver the concrete proposal and identify the
remaining dependency. Do not change the study's scientific claims merely to sound
more agreeable to a reviewer.

## Delivery

For review, provide prioritized major/minor concerns with actionable remedies and
an evidence-limited readiness assessment. For revision, provide the requested
response letter and revised artifacts, an honest change map, and unresolved issues.
Use `kite-literature`, `kite-experiments` or `kite-writing` when available; otherwise
perform the focused source, statistics or prose check directly.
