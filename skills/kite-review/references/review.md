# Review and response protocol / 审查与回复协议

## Concern record

| Location | Concern/status | Evidence | Consequence for claim | Minimal remedy | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Prioritize invalid inference, unsupported novelty, data leakage, unfair comparisons,
wrong replication units and citation misrepresentation over cosmetic edits. When
judging feasibility, tie the remedy to the central claim rather than demanding
every possible dataset, seed or sensitivity grid.

## Focused statistics audit

Check experimental unit, independence, paired/clustered structure, n and missingness,
effect definition, interval/test assumptions, multiplicity and figure labels. Verify
numbers against supplied artifacts. Statistical significance does not establish
mechanism or practical importance; an equivalence claim requires an appropriate design.

## Point-by-point response record

```text
Reviewer / Comment ID:
Original concern:
Direct response:
Action and status: proposed | implemented | verified
Evidence artifact:
Manuscript section / verified line or figure:
Remaining limitation or reasoned disagreement:
```

Quote supplied reviewer text as needed; do not import unverified external reviews.
When line numbers will change, use verified sections/figure identifiers until final
pagination is available. If a requested experiment would not resolve the concern,
explain why and propose an informative alternative with evidence.

## Final consistency

Reconcile every reviewer point with an actual response. Compare manuscript changes
to the response claims, verify reported analyses/figures and bibliography links,
and inspect rendered deliverables if files are produced. Keep unresolved concerns
explicit and bounded. Do not label a manuscript journal-ready just because all
review comments have corresponding paragraphs.
